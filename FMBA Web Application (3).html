For Those Managing the Final Journey

If death occurs at home:

Contact your doctor immediately to obtain a death certificate before taking any further step.

If death is due to an accident or involves legal matters:

Call 000 and follow the police instructions carefully before moving the deceased.

If death occurs in a hospital or healthcare facility:

Wait for the institution to issue the medical death certificate before proceeding with transport.

For burial arrangements:

  • Contact your preferred Funeral Director.
  • Inform them of the time and location for the Janazah (funeral) prayer.
  • If you already have a purchased burial plot, provide the plot number.
  • Advise the Funeral Director of your preferred cemetery for burial.

Islamic Rulings & Advice

Preparation & Islamic FAQs

Preparation for the Final Journey — Back to Our Creator

1. Repent Sincerely

Turn to Allah in sincere Tawbah (repentance). Regret sins, stop them immediately, and resolve never to return.

"And turn to Allah in repentance..." (Qur'an 24:31)

2. Seek Forgiveness from Others

Apologize and seek pardon from anyone you may have wronged. Return any rights or belongings owed.

"Settle matters before a Day when there will be neither dinar nor dirham." (Bukhari)

3. Clear All Debts & Obligations

Repay all debts or clearly document them.

"The soul of the believer is held by his debt until it is paid off." (Tirmidhi)

4. Fulfil Your Duties to Allah

Perform Hajj and Zakat if due and within your means. Leave clear instructions if unfulfilled.

5. Reconcile & Maintain Family Ties

Make peace with anyone you hold enmity toward. Strengthen ties with relatives.

6. Do More Good Deeds

Increase in charity, kindness, and remembrance of Allah. Even a smile is charity.

7. Write Your Will (Wasiyyah)

Prepare a written will including debts owed, Shari'ah inheritance distribution, and burial instructions.

8. Pray for Forgiveness & a Good Ending

Recite Sayyid al-Istighfar regularly asking Allah for refuge and a noble end.
